Launch of MAU ID

We have launched MAU ID — our own identification and authentication system within the MAU Accounts DAO ecosystem

Published on 26 June 2025 by MAU Accounts DAO

MAU ID identification authentication ecosystem decentralization

2 min read

We have launched MAU ID — our own identification and authentication system within the MAU Accounts DAO ecosystem.

This project was not created as an attempt to invent yet another “new internet”. Instead, it is a calm and deliberate response to what digital identity has increasingly become over recent years. More and more often, the same model is offered: a centralized provider, a single key “for everything”, extensive data collection, and a gradual loss of user control over their own digital identity.

MAU ID is built on different principles.

We deliberately do not follow the path of hyper-centralization and do not aim to become a universal identity provider for everyone. MAU ID is part of an autonomous, network-oriented model in which identity remains a tool of the user, not a lever of external control. This is not protocol-level or blockchain-driven decentralization. It is decentralization of a different kind — organizational and value-based — expressed through system architecture, access rules, and the way data is treated.

From a practical perspective, MAU ID addresses very down-to-earth needs. Users can register, sign in to ecosystem services, and manage their sessions and authentication methods using familiar and understandable mechanisms — usernames, passwords, and modern access keys. No biometric data. No dependency on global identity monopolies. No hidden conditions.

Under the hood, MAU ID is built on a mature, industrial-grade, open solution that has been proven over time and real-world usage. We intentionally chose reliability over experimentation, because identity is not an area for technological adventurism. It is a foundation on which trust is built.

In a broader sense, MAU ID can be seen as one of the bridges between Web 2.0 and Web 3.0. It allows people to live in the familiar web, use ordinary websites and services, while gradually moving away from models of total external control toward more autonomous, user-oriented systems.

It is important to emphasize that MAU ID is not designed solely for our internal projects. We are open to offering it to community members and independent projects that want to use MAU ID on their own websites, applications, and services as a ready-to-use foundation for secure, comprehensive, and manageable identity and access management.

With MAU ID, you can:

  • use an independent identifier that is not tied to global corporations,
  • benefit from a mature, production-grade system without having to build and maintain complex infrastructure yourself,
  • retain control over access rules, security policies, and the lifecycle of user accounts.

MAU ID is already in use within the MAU Accounts DAO ecosystem and will serve as a foundation for future services and projects. We continue to develop it carefully and without haste — in the way Cats prefer: stability first, scale second.
